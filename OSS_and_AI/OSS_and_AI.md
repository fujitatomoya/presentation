## How Open Source Traditionally Works

- Built on **voluntary collaboration** and **peer review**
- Maintainers ensure code quality and mentorship
- Contribution lifecycle: contributor → reviewer → maintainer
- Meritocracy and transparency as core values

## AI Enters the Workflow

- Rise of AI-assisted coding tools (e.g., Copilot, ChatGPT agents)
- Rapid code generation but **low contextual understanding**
- Surge in AI-generated Pull Requests (PRs)
- Authorship and accountability becoming **ambiguous**

*"1 out of 10 PRs created with AI is legitimate"* — Xavier Portilla Edo, Voiceflow

## The “Synthetic Senior” Phenomenon

- AI contributors appear *experienced* via fluent code
- Yet they **lack comprehension and maintainability**
- Maintainers face “review fatigue”
- Mentorship loop disrupted

💬 *Ref:* [FOSDEM 2026 – The Synthetic Senior](https://fosdem.org/2026/schedule/event/AJGB73-the_synthetic_senior_rethinking_free_software_mentorship_in_the_ai_era/)

## Maintainer Challenges

- Overload from **low-quality, auto-generated PRs**
- Difficulty verifying **license and origin**
- PRs often abandoned or violate contribution norms
- Human trust networks under stress

🧩 *Ref:* [GitHub community discussion #185387](https://github.com/orgs/community/discussions/185387)

## Platform-Level Responses (GitHub)

- “Kill switch” option to **temporarily disable PRs**
- Restrict PRs to collaborators or trusted users
- AI-content transparency and detection tools
- PR moderation automation

📰 *Ref:* [The Register, Feb 2026](https://www.theregister.com/2026/02/03/github_kill_switch_pull_requests_ai/)

## Community & Policy Adaptation

- Focus on **comprehension-based contribution tests**
- Introduce “effort friction” to filter spam PRs
- Self-serve onboarding & mentorship bots
- Accept AI use, but with disclosure & intent labeling

## Broader Risks

- Human learning value declining
- Metrics inflated by machine activity
- Trust and social capital erosion
- Burnout risk for maintainers

## Toward Sustainable AI–OSS Collaboration

- Combine **automation + mentorship**
- Leverage AI triage tools for review prioritization
- Preserve human mentorship for context and growth
- Promote **transparency, traceability, and accountability**

- AI expands capability but disrupts governance
- Maintainers must adapt policies & tooling
- Sustainable open source needs **AI-aware guidelines**
- Collaboration between platforms and communities is key
